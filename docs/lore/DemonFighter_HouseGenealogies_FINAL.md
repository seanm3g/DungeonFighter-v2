# DEMON FIGHTER — House Genealogies (Dray, Colehurst, Thorne, Marlow)

*Reference document. Split off from the combined Faerl Age draft so genealogy stays easy to find and update on its own — cross-references to specific historical events point to `DemonFighter_FaerlAge_HistoricalEvents_FINAL.md`. Builds on the locked Faerl Age era structure and the existing Antem-Extinction Overhaul canon. Where an entry touches an existing figure or event, it's marked (LOCKED) and left unmodified.*

**Founding principle:** all four Houses gain *Paramount* status simultaneously, ~1,000 years before Year Zero, when Varic Sennor's near-reunifying conquest fractures on his death (LOCKED — "Varic Sennor and the Last Crown"). His four surviving regional commanders divide his holdings along the lines their own armies already controlled. Colehurst alone carries a *pre-existing* lineage predating that moment by roughly 4,000 years (the unnamed First Forge-Debt ancestor, LOCKED, Early Rise era) — the other three founders are the first of their name to hold any real power. Generations are named at roughly 100–130 year intervals rather than every succession — consistent with the project's existing "degrading resolution" convention, more names cluster near the present. *BY = years before Year Zero.*

## House Dray (Desert) — "We do not thirst first"

| Gen. | Approx. BY | Name | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ~1,000 | **Kaspir Dray** | Founder. Claims the desert on the reasoning that only someone who already knows how to survive it can govern it. Before the division, swears **the Vow of the Last Crown** — that his line will one day finish what Varic Sennor started, not by conquering the other three Houses but by leading them back together against a shared enemy. Binding on his descendants by custom, not law; cited, consciously or not, every time a Dray paramount is first to commit troops to a joint cause (see Aden Dray, Casimir Dray, below). |
| 2 | ~900 | **Yorrin Dray** | Establishes the spoken-oath vassal tradition that defines Dray culture ever after. |
| 3 | ~790 | **Sella Dray** | First Dray Lady-Paramount. Repels an early Colehurst raid over a contested salt-flat — the seed of the Dray-Colehurst rivalry (see also: the Solvane Truce, Historical Events #48). |
| 4 | ~670 | **Barrow Dray** | Presides over House Centuries' worst famine; turns the "sealed waterskin" custom into formal law. |
| 5 | ~540 | **Ilvet Dray** | Dies without a son; daughter Ren inherits — the precedent later cited whenever a Dray heir needs defending. |
| 6 | ~500 | **Ren Dray** | Marries her daughter into House Colehurst — a fragile alliance that dies with her. |
| 7 | ~380 | **Varro Dray** | Loses two vassal holds to the Great Vantry Rift; never fully recovers Dray's southern border. |
| 8 | ~250 | **Aden Dray** | Enters the earliest coalition talks with Colehurst. |
| 9 | ~100 | **Casimir Dray** *(present at the Fall of Waelbrook)* | Commits Dray troops to the joint Colehurst-Thorne response — the coalition's real beginning. |
| 10 | ~10 BY–0 YOC | **Torvin Dray** *(LOCKED — Chronicle of Antarkeia)* | Leads Dray's forces at the Breach, alongside Beric Hallowmere (LOCKED, Dray-sworn, first to fall at the outer gate). |
| 11 | YT ~150–180 | **Kester Dray** *(LOCKED)* | Dies at the Ford at Wrenfield, thrown by a horse that stumbled on buried Antem tech. |
| 12 | YT 180–~230 | **Sorin Dray** | Kester's heir; presides over Dray's last stable decades. |
| — | past YT ~250 | *The line fragments* — no paramount remains, consistent with the present having no ruling Houses. |

## House Colehurst (Fire) — "First Law-blunt grimness"

*Ancient root: the unnamed First Forge-Debt ancestor (LOCKED, ~5,100 BY, Early Rise) — a hundred-generations-deep forge-debt network no other House can claim.*

| Gen. | Approx. BY | Name | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ~1,000 | **Rennick Colehurst** | Founder as Paramount; formalizes the ancestral forge-debt network into written law. |
| 2 | ~880 | **Doran Colehurst** | First written forge-debt contract — direct ancestor of every later Colehurst overreach. |
| 3 | ~760 | **Ysolt Colehurst** | Refuses to collect debts during a famine, breaking two centuries of precedent; her name becomes a minor Colehurst insult for "soft." |
| 4 | ~640 | **Harn Colehurst** | Presides over the founding of the battle-wizard tradition the later Sun Priest order (LOCKED) claims descent from. |
| 5 | ~510 | **Verity Colehurst** | Marries into House Dray (see Ren Dray, above); the alliance doesn't survive her. |
| 6 | ~390 | **Bram Colehurst** | Loses forge-territory to the Great Vantry Rift; rebuilds within a generation. |
| 7 | ~260 | **Fenric Colehurst** | Enters the earliest coalition talks with Dray. |
| 8 | ~100 | **Joran Colehurst** *(present at the Fall of Waelbrook)* | Responds jointly with Dray — the coalition's founding act, from Colehurst's side. |
| 9 | ~10 BY–0 YOC | **Harroc Colehurst** *(LOCKED — Chronicle of Antarkeia)* | Leads Colehurst's forces and his bound battle-wizards at the Breach; Roswen Ashcairn (LOCKED) serves under him with the fire-forges. |
| 10 | YT ~15–90 | **Aldous Colehurst** *(LOCKED)* | The Dominion — briefly the largest territory held since Consolidation, then collapses from overreach. |
| — | YT ~85–90 | **Garrick Forgehand** *(LOCKED, not Colehurst by blood)* | Leads the internal collapse that ends the Dominion. |
| 11 | YT 90–~140 | **Norah Colehurst** | Inherits a diminished House; Colehurst's signatory at the Concord of the Four Hearths (YT104, LOCKED). |
| — | past YT ~220 | *The line fragments.* |
