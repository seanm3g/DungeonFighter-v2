# DEMON FIGHTER
## Addendum: Cragwings (formerly "Ashwing Harpies") — Antarkeia's Sentinel Birds

*As with everything in this project: a breadcrumb, not canon set in stone.*

---

## What changed

The Chronicle of Antarkeia and the Ten Who Were There stub for Orune Featherfall both
originally used "Ashwing harpies" for the sentinel birds nesting in the high broken stone
above the gathering-grounds. That name collides with an unrelated entry already locked in
the `ENEMIES` spreadsheet: **Ashwing Harpy** — Fire region, Ashen Land biome, Smoldering
Ridge location, Rare rarity, Acrobat archetype, "a winged scavenger of volcanic skies —
fast, shrieking dives."

These are now two separate creatures. The spreadsheet entry is untouched and carries no
connection to Antarkeia. The Chronicle's sentinel birds are renamed **Cragwings**
everywhere they appear.

## Where "Cragwings" now applies

**In the Chronicle of Antarkeia**, under "A Ground That Was Never Only Ground":

> Cragwings nested in the high broken stone above it, and Antem hunters had given them a
> wide, respectful berth for generations.

**In the Ten Who Were There**, the Orune Featherfall stub:

> **Orune Featherfall — the harpy that didn't attack** — Cragwings screamed at every
> approaching force except, several accounts claim, one scouting party on the sixth day.
> Its leader called it luck. Antem tellings call it something closer to a verdict.

(Stub title kept as-is for now — "the harpy that didn't attack" still reads fine as a
folk nickname even with the species renamed underneath it. Revisit if it ever feels like
it's pointing back at the wrong name.)

## Why this split matters

Ashwing Harpy (spreadsheet) stays exactly what it's always been: a mechanical Fire-zone
combat entry, no lore obligation attached.

Cragwings (Chronicle) keep everything they already had — nesting site, generations of
Antem deference, and the Orune ambiguity (luck vs. verdict) that models the Chronicle's
unresolved-plurality device at the smallest possible scale. Nothing about their behavior,
range, or meaning changes — only the name, so the two no longer read as the same animal
by accident.
