**Demon Fighter — Antem-Extinction Overhaul — Decision Log**

*Living document. Read this first in any new session touching this overhaul before assuming anything about current canon. Update it as new decisions lock.*

**THE CORE CHANGE**

Antem (name likely to change — leading alternatives discussed: "The Others," "Them," othering-based naming) are extinct roughly 4,000–6,000 years before Year Zero. No living Antem, no Faerl/Antem coexistence, at any point close to the present era. They were the original inhabitants, built Antarkeia, used Vessels/androids, had a real deep history (civil wars, plagues, containment of demons) — none of that is erased, it's just pushed into unreachable, legend-only prehistory, LOST/Prometheus-style. All existing deep history content is being preserved and relocated, not deleted, wherever possible.

**THE FULL ARC — GENESIS TO PRESENT (locked narrative spine, 2026-09-11 clarification)**

The complete chain, in order. This supersedes any looser or partial framing of the same events elsewhere in this document — treat this section as the master reference.

1. **Deep Genesis.** Humans send robots and androids to this alien world (Ark 1). The Antem domesticate and leverage them, building an advanced, near-Atlantean society.
2. **Earth's mistake.** Humans back on Earth judge the colony a failure once the Antem evolve beyond what was anticipated — an unplanned evolution that raises Earth's own anxieties about being surpassed or made inferior. In response — a very human failure of foresight and oversight, not calculated cruelty — Earth extracts emotions and bombards the planet with the resulting vials, intending to destroy the Antem. These vials are what eventually become demons.
3. **Misread as a gift.** The Antem, with no way to understand the bombardment's hostile origin, interpret it as some kind of gift they don't fully understand.
4. **The gardener's error.** Over generations, the Antem tend what emerges from the vials the way a gardener tends overgrowth — cutting it back, believing they're containing it. Critically, they never fight the demons. That passive-containment posture, not any external force, is their actual mistake.
5. **Corruption, not conquest.** Sustained proximity corrupts the Antem across that same span, driving their internal civil wars, the plagues (the Hollowing), the Stillwing departure, and the rest of their long decline — all of it already detailed in the Hollowing/Rivening document. This is the only version of Antem deep history that exists; Faerl in the present only ever access it through relics, ruins, and artifacts, never firsthand.
6. **Minor crossover.** Rare, small-scale contact occurs between Antem and still-primitive Faerl during this span — the already-locked sparse-contact principle below.
7. **Self-inflicted extinction.** The Antem's own corruption, not any outside conqueror, eventually drives them fully extinct. They have nothing to blame but themselves.
8. **Faerl expansion.** With the Antem gone and the "garden" now unchecked, Faerl — formerly just sea-people from another continent — expand into and colonize the continent, naming it Faerlan. (What the Antem themselves called it is unknown/unrecorded.)
9. **The Age of Man.** Over the following centuries, Faerl civilization rises — Game-of-Thrones-flavored wars, conflicts, and ruling houses rising and falling. Magic is present but has become rare, largely legendary-tier, during this era: the Antem's containment work, undone by their own corruption but still mechanically running down rather than gone, has done an "okay job" of keeping the garden pruned this whole time — that time is drawing to a close, but hasn't ended yet.
10. **Consolidation.** Containment finally runs out enough, and Faerl civilization consolidates enough, to mount a coordinated attempt on Antarkeia.
11. **Year Zero.** Antarkeia is breached. The demons are fully re-released into the world.
12. **Disintegration.** The medieval structure that carried Faerl through their rise does not survive contact with the unleashed demons — it disintegrates gradually, not overnight.
13. **The present.** Not a collapse into chaos so much as a genuine regression — a fallen era with no ruling houses left to hold things together, where the fate of Faerlish society could still swing either direction, upward or downward, and that direction is deliberately left unwritten. Working names under consideration, not committed: "The Age Unwritten," "The Unbound Age." Tonally closer to Roland's "the world has moved on" (Dark Tower) than to outright Mad Max collapse.

**Why this structure matters (stated design intent):** it produces deep, rich backstory without creating present-day open questions. Players don't need an explanation for why they can move through the world with impunity, because there's no intact political structure left to explain around. Whether ordinary Faerl in the present even know their own deep history is left open — like medieval Europeans and Rome, they may or may not — and that ambiguity is fine. All of the game's mystery should come from relics, ruins, and artifacts, not from unresolved present-day politics.

**LOCKED WORLDBUILDING DECISIONS**

- Antem were reluctant "gardener" figures who contained/managed demons for generations and were corrupted by that proximity — this is their downfall, a "smallpox blanket" irony (thought the demons were a gift). **Reaffirmed:** this corrosion is the deep, generations-long cause of Antem decline; the ~72-year Hollowing/Rivening/Fragmentation arc (below) is the acute, final collapse sitting on top of that much longer decay, not a standalone cause. Antem outward power/dominance stayed intact and visible to outsiders (including a still-marginal Faerl) right up until the Hollowing made the underlying rot visible.
- Faerl/Antem contact was never population-scale. Sparse, individual, sea-people-meets-bronze-age-style crossover only, confined to deep prehistory before Antem's extinction. **Standing principle (saved to memory): any story implying large-scale interbreeding or population-level Faerl/Antem consequences is invalid and should be cut or reworked, not just relocated in time** — population-scale outcomes require population-scale contact, which never happened.
- Year Zero (0 YT, name TBD — "Triumph" is dead, "The Fall" / "Era of Reclamation" under consideration, not committed) is NOT a battle. It's Faerls converging on and breaching a long-abandoned Antarkeia — more mummy's-tomb than siege. No living defenders. Whatever resistance exists is automated/environmental. **Now written into canon** — see Chronicle rewrite below.
- Antarkeia is a hub connected by invisible "spokes" to satellite containment structures across the continent (Dark Tower/Guardians-style). Breaching the hub causes a visible, continent-wide lightning-like event revealing the spoke sites — this explains why demon-related ruins/hotspots are scattered all over Faerlan, not just at Antarkeia. **Now formally written into `DemonFighter_Antarkeia_Addendum_and_Geography_REVISED.md`'s Part Two, tied to Antarkeia's existing position as the continent's geographic nexus.**
- The ~72-year Hollowing → Rivening → Fragmentation arc (plague, Stillwing extinction, civil war, total collapse) is the right "whimper not bang" decline story and is being KEPT — it now runs roughly 5,072 to 5,000 years before Year Zero (shifted per the ~5,000-year working estimate), ending long before Year Zero instead of right at it.
- The Hollowing's origin trigger (a Faerl handler + Antem travelers disturbing sealed ground together) is KEPT, and the passage's existing ambiguity about who is actually responsible is KEPT AS-IS — deliberately never resolved which party (or neither) caused the release. Faerl involvement should read as hapless/accidental, not villainous or narratively culpable for the Antem's extinction. This is ancient, unconfirmed legend by the standards of the game's present and should stay that way.
- Modern Faerl "legends" about the Antem are literally legend — Odyssey-style oral tradition from the ancient sparse-contact period, filtered through millennia of myth-drift. Not folk memory of a recently-departed people.
- **The ~5,000-year gap's arc** — see "THE FULL ARC — GENESIS TO PRESENT" at the top of this document for the complete, current version. (The version of this bullet previously here used "collapse into chaos" for the post-breach era; that's been softened and superseded — see point 13 above.)

**SPECIES DECISIONS**

- **Antem** — extinct, ruins/relics/pictographs/legend only. Rewritten in Species Canon.
- **Kin** — CUT ENTIRELY. No remnant, no legend, never existed in this version of canon. No hook, created more questions than answers.
- **Reachborn** — CUT ENTIRELY (old name, already superseded/folded into the Kin cut and Farhand rework).
- **Vessel** — EXTINCT (changed from old canon's "nearly extinct, a handful remain"). Confirmed direction: Vessels SHOULD appear prominently in murals/pictographs/ruin-art as a mysterious servant class that appears to have been human — deliberate mystery-fuel, not an oversight. **Note:** under the shifted timeline, Vessels are already extinct well before Year Zero (their decline runs alongside the Antem's per the Hollowing/Rivening document) — no living Vessels at Antarkeia itself. The Chronicle rewrite reflects this.
- **Empty** — RETIRED as its own species/lore category. Folded into the existing Bestiary Zombie entry. Players fight zombies, never "Empties," no in-world name or exposition. The Zombie entry should absorb: organic (not mechanical) deterioration, patchy discoloration, and the strongest detail — a face still holding the ghost of an approachable designed expression. Never explained in-world.
- **Farhand** — KEPT, reworked. No longer literal Faerl/Antem interbreeding (impossible now). Recast as Faerl-only rootless underclass whose claimed Antem ancestry is unverifiable self-mythology/folklore — the "I've got Indian blood" analogy. Naming/culture (two-word spirit names) unchanged.
- **Non** — REVISED, not cut. Redefined as Ark 1 descendants, same species as Faerl (a "breed," like pug vs. greyhound, not a separate species), secretive knowledge-caste present in some capacity at nearly every major historical event, ambiguous motives, deliberately shadowy.
- **Pale Maret** — CUT (not repurposed). Removed from character roster and tavern vignette in Reference.docx.
- **Wicked Wanda, Finch** (Reference.docx roster/vignette characters, formerly Kin) — reassigned to Farhand.
- **Sarel of the Long Watch** (Chronicle of Antarkeia vignette) — reassigned from Kin to Faerl. Fixed same-session as the Chronicle rewrite below.
- **Iskrel Sixhold** (Scattering Wars, in Deep History and the Calendar/Faiths/Houses document) — was flagged as "currently Kin, reassignment needed (Faerl likely)." **Fixed and corrected 2026-09-11: reassigned to Antem, not Faerl.** Deep History's own relocated Skirmish at Yalume Ford entry explicitly ties Iskrel Sixhold's early tactics to a fight "between two Antem clans," confirming Antem over the earlier guess. Also fixed in the same pass: the Calendar document's claim that the Unbroken Terms "persist in fragments among some Kin communities today" — Kin is cut entirely, and no version of this could persist as living tradition among any present-day community; it now survives only as inherited Faerl legend, same as the rest of Antem history.

**PROCESS NOTES / LESSONS LEARNED THIS SESSION**

- Semantic/keyword search (`project_knowledge_search`) can silently miss files, especially when two files share an exact filename — only one is visible to file-system tools in that case. Full audits should enumerate the directory directly and read/grep each file, not rely on search hits alone.
- Before proposing a specific date/species swap on any individual historical entry, check its actual BYT math against the locked ~5,000-year shift rather than eyeballing "this feels old enough" — a prior attempt to do this on the fly (BYT 2980 → Antem) was wrong and had to be corrected.
- When deleting any project file, verify a byte-identical backup exists and has been confirmed downloaded before deletion — never delete on the same turn a fix is proposed.
- Do not batch multiple asks/decisions into one message during this work — one step at a time, confirm, then proceed.
- A file that touches the old battle narrative can't be fully fixed in isolation — the Hollowing/Rivening rewrite hit this directly (Sections V–IX depend on the Chronicle). Worth checking any remaining file for this same dependency before starting its edit, not partway through.
- **New this session:** re-uploading a file with the exact same name as one already in the project can silently create a duplicate with `(1)`/`__1_` appended instead of overwriting it, leaving the old, wrong version still live and indistinguishable at a glance. Delete the old file first, *then* upload the replacement, rather than uploading first and deleting after — and always verify the live file's actual content post-upload, not just that a file with the right name exists.
