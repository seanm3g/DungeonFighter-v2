## PART TWO — WORLD GEOGRAPHY

Five landmasses, established this session, replacing any earlier vague or unplaced continent references. **Water is not a sixth landmass** — the `ENVIRONMENTS` sheet confirms "water" is a cross-cutting tag (ice caverns, swamps, oases, springs) rather than a standalone biome or dungeon theme. Ocean sits as connective tissue between and around everything; Antarkeia sits at the geographic and thematic nexus where all four of Faerlan's biome influences meet.

### Compass mapping

| Direction | Faerlan-facing biome | Neighboring continent |
|---|---|---|
| North | Ice / frost | Ossmoor |
| East | Forest | Kaeruvai |
| South | Desert | Kessara |
| West | Fire / volcanic | Vantry Isles |

### Faerlan — the central continent

Not four hard quadrants meeting at right angles — an irregular, elongated landmass with a central highland spine, from which four environmental influences radiate outward and **blend into one another at their borders** rather than snapping to a boundary line. Working transitional zones, none yet formally named:

- North meeting East — frost-touched boreal pine forest, taiga
- East meeting South — dry woodland thinning into savanna/scrubland
- South meeting West — cracked badlands, dark volcanic sand
- West meeting North — a geothermal fire-and-ice zone, black volcanic rock steaming under snow, Iceland-flavored

Rivers originate in the central highlands and cut irregular paths to the coast. Where the highlands lower into wetland/lake country and all four influences overlap, Antarkeia sits — not at a mathematically exact center, but naturally within a lowland basin, partially reclaimed by marsh.

**Antarkeia as hub, not just nexus (locked this pass).** The geographic centrality above turns out to be functional, not just thematic: Antarkeia is the hub of an invisible network of "spoke" containment structures scattered across the continent, Dark Tower/Guardians-style — the same automated systems already described in the Hollowing/Rivening document (Section VIII) as having kept demons mostly suppressed for the ~5,000 years between Antem extinction and the breach. Breaching the hub is what finally ends that containment, continent-wide, in a single visible event — accounts describe something like lightning without a storm, radiating outward from Antarkeia toward the horizon in every direction at once, seen and remembered even by people who had no idea what they were watching. It's the retroactive explanation for why demon-related ruins and hotspots are scattered all over Faerlan rather than clustered near Antarkeia alone — every one of them is a spoke, not an independent occurrence. None of this should be stated in-world as mechanism; it's creator knowledge that keeps future content consistent, the same way the automated-containment logic in the Hollowing/Rivening document is.

### Ossmoor — north

An icy landmass stretching across the full northern edge, understood to continue beyond what's ever been charted — the "Antarctica at the bottom of a real-world map" effect, just inverted to the top. Jagged ice-shelf coastline, glacial crevasse fields, at least one long peninsula reaching toward Faerlan.

### Kaeruvai — east

A large forested continent, understood to extend beyond any charted edge — the "Essos" effect. Tapering, asymmetrical silhouette; dense rainforest canopy, deep river systems, a mountain range along one coast. Standing legend (unconfirmed) holds this is where the departed Stillwings may have found other riders.

### Kessara — south

A smaller, fully self-contained desert continent, genuinely separate from Faerlan with open ocean on all sides — not a landmass that trails off-map like Ossmoor or Kaeruvai. Arid interior, central mountain range, dry riverbeds, scattered oases.

### The Vantry Isles — west

Restructured this session from a blobby archipelago into a curved island chain — Japan/Indonesia-flavored, elongated and irregular islands strung along a natural arc, varying sizes, volcanic and rocky.

**The Great Vantry Rift** (name locked this session, replacing earlier "Vantry Break"): the isles were once a single sixth continent. Physical evidence a landmass once stood there is undisputed — sea-floor depth, matching rock strata across islands now a day's sail apart, and charts older than any dateable record showing a coastline that no longer exists. What's unrecoverable is *why*, and how large it originally was — the cause predates the oldest surviving record on either side, and nothing that could carry a firsthand account survived it. Favored (not confirmed) explanation: failure of some extraordinarily old, dense piece of pre-collapse technology, not a curse or magical doom — deliberately distinct from a "Doom of Valyria" framing. The isles are harsh, geothermally active, and hard to live on — but genuinely inhabited, not a wasteland. Faerlan's existing Fire-zone enemies (Fire Elemental, Lava Golem, Salamander, Ember Spirit, Magma Beast) are proposed as descending from or migrating out of whatever still smolders in the Rift's remnants.
