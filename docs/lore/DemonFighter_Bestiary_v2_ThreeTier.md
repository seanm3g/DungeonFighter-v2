# DEMON FIGHTER
## The Bestiary, Version Two — Three Kinds of Creature

*A breadcrumb, not canon set in stone. Supersedes the prior full-bestiary draft. The change:*
*not every creature carries a sci-fi echo anymore. Most don't. This document sorts all 127*
*entries into three honest categories and writes each one according to which it belongs to.*

---

## THE THREE KINDS

**Pure native fauna.** Evolved on The First Child, full stop. No Earth ancestry, no tech
underneath, nothing to infer. These should read as simply, warmly *real* — odd by our
standards sometimes, never uncanny by the world's own.

**Feral Earth-stock.** Animals that arrived domesticated, almost certainly aboard Ark 1 given
the transit timescale, and have spent tens of thousands of years drifting back toward
wildness in a world that had two full ages to forget they were ever anything else. These carry
a specific warmth: not mystery, but a faint, structural memory of having once been cared for.
Old herding instinct that never quite finished dying. A hesitation at a raised hand that no truly
wild thing should have.

**Techno-echo / Genesis-adjacent.** Constructs, undead, spirits, mimics, and the handful of
"real" creatures load-bearing enough to keep a wink — mostly things already tied to the
Great Vantry Rift's lineage, or things that were never really animals to begin with. This
category stays exactly as rare as it needs to be to still feel like a discovery.

The faction framework from the last draft still applies underneath all of this — the Bound,
the Boneyard-trained, the glory-hunters, the desperate — and gets folded in wherever it
earns its place, not as a formula repeated on every entry.

# I. THE GENERAL RECKONING

**Goblin** — *Genesis-adjacent.* Small, hunched, quick to violence. Old Faerl belief holds
these were once ordinary men — usually poor, usually desperate — who went looking for a
demon's wealth without asking what it costs to carry. What came back wasn't quite a man
and wasn't quite anything else. Their eyes hold a color nothing else in this world quite
matches. The Bound kill without ceremony. Wizards are the ones most likely to hesitate.

**Spider** — *Pure native fauna.* A large, patient forest hunter, its web strong enough to
hold a grown man's weight and its venom fast-acting enough that most victims never feel
the second bite. Common folk kill them on sight out of plain, sensible fear — nothing more
mysterious required.

**Wolf** — *Feral Earth-stock.* Hunts in loose, cooperative packs with a discipline that
outlives what a truly wild predator needs — flanks that hold, signals that get answered. Old
herders swear a cornered pack will sometimes hesitate at a raised hand the way nothing wild
ever should. Nobody teaches them that anymore. Something in the blood still remembers
being taught, once, by someone who never came back to finish the job. Barbarians hold a
genuine, wordless respect for them — not fear, something closer to recognizing kin who
forgot they were kin.

**Fire Elemental** — *Techno-echo.* Burns a flame that never gutters in wind, holding a
faint violet undertone no honest hearth-fire has ever produced. Coastal Faerl tell their
children these climbed up out of a wound in the world that never fully closed — the same
breath in which most of them insist that wound doesn't exist.

**Lava Golem** — *Techno-echo.* A slow, massive fusion of molten stone around a core too
regular in shape to be geology's doing. Boneyard quartermasters price its remains high; what's
inside the chest sometimes survives the kill still faintly warm, days later, in a satchel.

**Bat** — *Pure native fauna.* Swarms through total cave-dark in numbers thick enough to
sound like weather. Cave-guides who work by torch alone learn to trust their footing more
than their eyes down here regardless of what's flying past them.

**Skeleton** — *Genesis-adjacent.* Rings like struck metal under a blade, never quite
splintering the way old, brittle bone should. The Bound consider these among the clean kills
— nothing left to grieve. Everyone else just calls it easy coin.

**Zombie** — *Genesis-adjacent.* Decays slower and wronger than flesh should, hollowed
from within in a way that echoes — a little too closely for comfortable conversation — old,
half-forgotten stories of a plague nobody currently living was alive to see end. Mothers in
some river communities still won't speak that plague's name over a fresh grave.

**Wraith** — *Genesis-adjacent.* A drifting shape the exact flat grey of an old, private grief
— and travelers from opposite ends of the continent, who've never met, describe the same
specific shade without fail.

**Bear** — *Pure native fauna.* A genuinely formidable forest predator, heavy-bodied and
short-tempered, that dens where the terrain lets it and defends that den without hesitation.
Barbarians hold these in real, earned respect — a bear beaten is a story worth keeping; a
bear avoided, wisely, is a story nobody tells but everyone who lived understands.

**Treant** — *Genesis-adjacent.* Split one open, on the rare occasion its bulk permits it, and
the rings inside don't spiral the way a tree's rings should — they nest, tight and deliberate,
like something planned rather than grown.

**Boar** — *Feral Earth-stock.* A heavy, bristling brute that charges before it thinks — but
corner one somewhere enclosed instead of open woods and it'll sometimes simply stop,
confused, rooting at ground that was never a trough. Old farming families in the deeper wilds
still swap the same tired joke: that somewhere back before anyone's grandfather's
grandfather, something like this lived in a pen and fed from a hand and slept warm. Nobody
really believes it. Nobody's quite let it go, either.

**Salamander** — *Techno-echo.* A fire-dwelling lizard whose bloodline, according to the
oldest western fishing families, traces back to whatever still smolders beneath the sea west
of Faerlan — kin, distant and diminished, to whatever failed catastrophically out there long
before anyone had names for continents.

**Ember Spirit** — *Techno-echo.* Bursts in pulses too rhythmically even to be a flame's
natural flicker — coastal fisherfolk call it "the tide's twin," and mean it as observation more
than metaphor.

**Magma Beast** — *Techno-echo.* The molten glow beneath its stone hide flares the
identical shade whether calm or raging, as though something underneath holds the color
steady on purpose.

**Ghoul** — *Genesis-adjacent.* Goes for whatever a corpse carries before it goes for the
corpse itself — a hunger old battlefield-scavengers insist isn't the kind anyone recognizes.

**Lich** — *Genesis-adjacent.* Always clutches some small object in a fist that never opens.
The rare hunter who's pried one loose describes it as warm, worked in a material no smith
present could name.

**Wight** — *Genesis-adjacent.* Guards one exact point within a crypt, precise to the
footstep, as if the boundary was drawn for it long before it ever took up the post.

# II. AIR — THE DESERT ZONE

*Proven Right country in the making. Settlers rarely mention the Antem who once knew*
*these dunes; the doctrine doesn't require them to.*

### Dune

**Sandstorm Flanker** — *Pure native fauna.* A lean desert raider that cuts in low from the
edge of a storm, using the blinding sand the way any smart predator uses weather — as
cover, nothing more mystical than that.

**Sand Serpent** — *Techno-echo.* Sleek, faintly metallic scales that catch the light wrong
for something that lives buried in sand — old handlers half-joke it was forged, not hatched,
and half of them aren't joking.

**Dune Roller** — *Techno-echo.* Curls into a shape too perfectly round for shell or bone.
The dunes here hum, some nights, in a spot no one's ever dug to the bottom of.

### Glass Plains

**Sun Lance Scorpion** — *Pure native fauna.* A large, sun-armored scorpion whose tail
gleams like a spearhead in direct light — precise, poisonous, and entirely its own doing.

**Glare Stalker** — *Pure native fauna.* A hunter that's learned to use the plain's own
blinding reflections as camouflage — a real, teachable trick of the local light, not a mystery.

**Glass Shard Golem** — *Techno-echo.* Fused from the same glass the plain itself is made
of. The old desert saying is that the same fire that made this ground made this creature too.

### Oasis

**Mirage Hunter** — *Pure native fauna.* Hunts by false shapes in the heat-haze, a trick of
timing and stillness any patient predator in open desert eventually learns.

**Shadow Wraith** — *Genesis-adjacent.* A shade born of desert noon, phasing through
light the exact way old tellings describe a certain ancient sickness taking someone from the
inside.

**Restorative Leech** — *Pure native fauna.* A parasitic desert leech that feeds and mends
itself with remarkable efficiency — sustains through stolen life, same as any leech anywhere,
just better at it than most.

### Pyramids — Grand Corridor

**Stone Colossus** — *Techno-echo.* Carved sandstone jointed at points no statue needs
joints, standing exactly where every account for generations says it's always stood.

**Poison Dart Sentinel** — *Techno-echo.* Fires from stillness with a timing too consistent
across generations of tomb-robbers to be one guardian's individual skill.

**Sun Priest** — *Genesis-adjacent.* A desert cleric who channels solar wrath with
a heat that burns the same pale color every casting. Old fire-doctrine holds this is what true
flame looked like, once.

### Pyramids — Tomb Entrance

**Guardian Scarab** — *Pure native fauna.* A sacred beetle grown to impossible size, its
shell segmented and fiercely territorial — bred for generations by tomb-keepers to be
exactly this large and exactly this stubborn.

**Tomb Creeper** — *Pure native fauna.* A corpse-thief that navigates burial-passage dark
with the confident ease of something that's lived nowhere else for a very long time.

**Sarcopha Gus** — *Genesis-adjacent.* Bound to a sarcophagus older than the tomb built
to house it — whatever's sealed within has outlasted every dynasty that came after.

### Pyramids — Burial Chamber

**Burial Lich** — *Genesis-adjacent.* Commands death with a precision that reads less like
sorcery and more like memory — an old, exact knowledge nobody living still carries in full.

**Mummy** — *Genesis-adjacent.* Preserved well past any decay flesh should be capable of
resisting. The wrappings are ceremony. They are not what's keeping it whole.

**Ancestor's Shade** — *Genesis-adjacent.* Strikes with grudges too specific, too personal,
to belong to any dynasty anyone's ever managed to document by name.

### Mountains — Cliffs Edge

**Hawk** — *Feral Earth-stock.* A desert raptor that dives from above with an accuracy
falconers find eerily cooperative to train — as though some old, patient part of it already
understands the command before it's given.

**Rock Lobber** — *Pure native fauna.* A brute that hurls sun-baked stones from a distance
— simple, crushing, and entirely its own considerable strength at work.

**Edge Stalker** — *Pure native fauna.* A blade-wielding hunter who circles the
rim of sight, patient enough to wait out anyone less disciplined than himself.

### Mountains — Abandoned Campsite

**Scavenger Rat** — *Feral Earth-stock.* A gnawing vermin of the wastes, quick and
opportunistic — descended, most likely, from stock that scattered wide and far over tens of
thousands of years and never stopped scavenging what it could.

**Toxic Drinker** — *Pure native fauna.* A mutated desert dweller that drinks poison to fuel
its own attacks — a real, if unpleasant, adaptation to a harsh place.

**Deserter Shade** — *Genesis-adjacent.* The ghost of a soldier who fled into the dunes
and never came back out. Bitter, elusive, and strangely specific about which direction he
insists he ran from.

### Mountains — The Summit

**Storm Caller** — *Pure native fauna.* A mountain mystic who summons thunder
and wind, casting from a distance with the confidence of someone who's done it for decades.

**Altitude Wraith** — *Genesis-adjacent.* A thin-air specter that haunts high passes, cold
and disorienting in a way travelers describe as feeling aimed, not incidental.

**Summit Titan** — *Techno-echo.* A giant of stone and ice at the mountain peak. Old
mountain-folk insist something this precisely shaped doesn't happen by accident of erosion
alone.

# III. FIRE — THE FIRE ZONE

*Two rival doctrines still argue over whether to conquer this ground or simply watch it.*
*Neither has stopped being afraid of it.*

### Ashen Land — Smoldering Ridge

**Cinder Hound** — *Feral Earth-stock.* A fire-scorched hound that hunts in bursts of
speed and fury — another branch of the same old, half-remembered lineage as the forest
wolves, drifted west long enough ago to forget it was ever anything but wild.

**Magma Creeper** — *Pure native fauna.* A lava-dwelling predator that crawls through
fissures, ambushing from below with the patience of something perfectly adapted to heat
most things can't survive.

**Ashwing Harpy** — *Pure native fauna.* A winged scavenger of volcanic skies, fast and
shrieking in its dives — an ordinary, if fearsome, predator of the high ash-currents. Unrelated
to anything else that watches from high stone elsewhere on this continent.

### Ashen Land — Smokey Cave

**Soot Golem** — *Techno-echo.* A construct of compressed ash and cinders, armored and
smothering, blocking passages with a deliberateness that reads like decision rather than
obstacle.

**Cave Ember Toad** — *Pure native fauna.* A bloated toad swollen with inner heat, lurking
and lunging from cave pools — a real, if uncomfortable, adaptation to this stretch of cave.

**Smog Lurker** — *Pure native fauna.* A predator hidden in volcanic fumes, striking only
once visibility fails completely.

### Ashen Land — Blackened Forest

**Charwood Stalker** — *Pure native fauna.* A burned-forest hunter moving through
blackened trunks, patient in the specific way a survivor of a bad fire learns to be.

**Scorched Treant** — *Genesis-adjacent.* A fire-blackened tree spirit, slow and thick-
barked, its rings nested in the same wrong, deliberate pattern found in every living treant
elsewhere — this one simply also burned.

**Reignited Wisp** — *Genesis-adjacent.* A flame spirit that flares back to life after every
kill, as though the fire it carries was never truly extinguished — a small, local echo of a much
larger miscast fire nobody discusses in polite company anymore.

### Fire Temple — Lava Moat Bridge

**Bridge Warden** — *Pure native fauna.* A lava-bridge guardian in scorched
armor, holding the line with a discipline that's clearly outlasted whoever gave the original
order.

**Lava Spitter** — *Pure native fauna.* A creature that spews molten rock from a distance
— a real elemental predator, ranged and dangerous on its own biological terms.

**Flame Drifter** — *Genesis-adjacent.* A wandering fire spirit that darts through heat
currents moving in patterns too structured to be simple convection doing what convection
does.

### Fire Temple — Torched Great Hall

**Temple Knight** — *Pure native fauna.* A zealot knight sworn to the flame
temple — armored, trained, and entirely mortal.

**Pillar Watcher** — *Techno-echo.* A sentinel posted beside temple pillars, still and alert
in a way that reads less like devotion than instruction.

**Torch Wraith** — *Genesis-adjacent.* A ghost bound to eternal flame, deceiving through
flickering light with a control the old sun-priests would recognize immediately as trained, not
chaotic.

### Fire Temple — Throne Room

**Royal Flame Guard** — *Pure native fauna.* An elite temple defender in
ceremonial armor — the ceremony has clearly outlived whoever it was originally for.

**Seal Caster** — *Pure native fauna.* A mage who binds enemies with fiery
sigils, technical and controlled.

**Throne Effigy** — *Techno-echo.* A stone guardian carved to sit before a molten throne,
immovable. Nobody's confirmed whether it was carved to guard the throne, or simply to
remember who once sat on it.

### Lava Cave — Lava Spring

**Blood Feeder** — *Pure native fauna.* A parasite that drains life from open wounds,
predatory and precise in the way any well-adapted parasite is.

**Vent Spore** — *Pure native fauna.* A fungal spore creature born from volcanic vents,
spreading harm through contact — strange biology, but biology all the same.

**Crystal Leech** — *Techno-echo.* A glassy parasite that latches to heated crystal and
hardens, pulsing in the same slow rhythm as crystal formations found in caves on the far side
of the continent — a resemblance nobody's connected on purpose, only noticed.

### Lava Cave — Slag Archipelago

**Slag Tortoise** — *Pure native fauna.* A turtle shelled in cooled slag, slow and extremely
tough — a real, if peculiar, adaptation to an archipelago made of cooling lava. Shares a name,
coincidentally, with something a mining town further north once watched surface, wait
nineteen days, and vanish underground again.

**Island Drifter** — *Pure native fauna.* A creature carried by thermal winds between lava
isles, fast and hard to pin down — a genuine flight adaptation to a genuinely strange
environment.

**Molten Crab** — *Pure native fauna.* A crab with a shell of hardened magma, armored
and snapping — tough in the way anything that lives here has learned to be tough.

### Lava Cave — Amber City

**Amber Sentinel** — *Techno-echo.* A guardian encased in ancient amber, preserved and
patient in a way that suggests waiting for something specific rather than merely standing
guard indefinitely.

**Overflow Imp** — *Genesis-adjacent.* A mischievous spirit of excess heat and pressure.
Old-timers say it's what escapes whenever something else, somewhere nearby, is being
successfully kept in.

**Amber Trapper** — *Pure native fauna.* A predator that lures prey into sticky amber
pools — a genuinely effective, entirely biological trapping strategy.

# IV. FOREST — THE FOREST ZONE

*Old, quiet ground. Barbarians who've spent real time here develop a wordless respect for it*
*that doesn't survive translation to anyone who's only ever fought in it.*

### Den — Cave Entrance

**Territorial Hound** — *Feral Earth-stock.* A fierce hound defending its hunting ground,
aggressive and pack-minded — cousin, distant and wilder, to the wolves that share this
forest's old, half-forgotten bloodline.

**Alpha Wolf** — *Feral Earth-stock.* The lead wolf of a border pack, fast, commanding,
and relentless — carrying that same faint, structural memory of a hand that once fed it,
generations upon generations removed.

**Bone Spreader** — *Genesis-adjacent.* A necromantic scavenger that scatters cursed
bones in configurations that repeat, generation after generation, in the exact same pattern.

### Den — Offering Mound

**Tribute Beast** — *Pure native fauna.* A bound creature left as offering to old powers
nobody living remembers the name of — heavy, savage, and enduring on its own considerable
merits.

**Cursed Bear** — *Genesis-adjacent.* A bear twisted by forest hexes, powerful and brutal
— an ordinary, respected predator made wrong by something laid over it rather than
anything native to the species itself.

**Mound Warden** — *Techno-echo.* A guardian of ancient burial mounds, armored and
territorial, that has plainly outlasted whoever originally built what it's guarding.

### Den — Vigil Circle

**Moon Blessed** — *Genesis-adjacent.* A forest spirit touched by moonlight, healing and
harming with sacred power that pulses in a rhythm too metronomic for anything as irregular
as a real lunar cycle.

**Echo Stalker** — *Pure native fauna.* A hunter that strikes from reflected sound, using
this forest's own strange, layered acoustics the way any well-adapted predator uses its
terrain.

**Vigil Alpha** — *Feral Earth-stock.* The commander of a night watch pack, leading with
strength and an uncanny, disciplined cunning — the clearest living example in this whole
forest of what that old, buried herding instinct looks like when it actually holds.

### Labyrinth — Hedge Corridor

**Guile Sprite** — *Genesis-adjacent.* A tiny fey spirit of trickery, fast and mischievous in
the old sense of the word — its unpredictability reading, on close observation, more like
malfunction than personality.

**Hedge Hound** — *Feral Earth-stock.* A lean hound hunting along maze hedges, quick
and flanking — another thread of that same old bloodline, gone lean and clever in tight
spaces.

**Dead End Mimic** — *Genesis-adjacent.* A shapeshifter that waits at false exits, lures
victims into ambush — always resembling, with uncanny specificity, the exact exit you were
hoping to find.

### Labyrinth — Endless Maze

**Maze Runner** — *Pure native fauna.* A creature born to sprint endless corridors —
blinding speed, low endurance, bred by this strange terrain into something built for exactly
this shape of space.

**Loop Shade** — *Genesis-adjacent.* A spirit trapped in repeating paths, disorienting
anyone who follows. The paths were already repeating before the shade arrived to haunt
them.

**Instinct Eater** — *Genesis-adjacent.* A horror that feeds on prey's fear and reflex,
preying specifically on hesitation.

### Labyrinth — Room of Two Mirrors

**Duplicate** — *Genesis-adjacent.* A mirror-image foe that copies every movement with a
fidelity too exact to be mimicry.

**Shattered Reflection** — *Genesis-adjacent.* A broken mirror spirit attacking through
fractured images that, on close inspection, don't actually match how the mirrors in this room
were ever physically broken.

**The Other** — *Genesis-adjacent.* A doppelganger wearing your own face — psychological
and deadly, and never explained beyond that, deliberately.

### Dense Brush — Thicket

**Thorn Boar** — *Feral Earth-stock.* A bristling boar wrapped in thorny hide, charging
with brutal force — kin, distant and wilder, to whatever once lived somewhere warmer and
smaller and fed from a hand.

**Root Sprite** — *Pure native fauna.* A small woodland spirit tied to living roots, nimble
and evasive — as native to this thicket as the roots it hides among.

**Rattlesnake** — *Pure native fauna.* A coiled viper that strikes without warning,
venomous and precise — a genuinely dangerous, entirely ordinary predator.

### Dense Brush — Wildflower Valley

**Nectar Drone** — *Pure native fauna.* An oversized pollinator driven to frenzy, fast and
swarming — strange in scale, not in kind.

**Pollen Wraith** — *Genesis-adjacent.* A drifting spirit of choking pollen, obscuring sight
and sapping strength — grief-grey, like every wraith-kin creature across the continent.

**Valley Prowler** — *Pure native fauna.* A lowland predator hunting at dusk, swift and
aggressive, timed to the fading light the way any patient hunter learns to be.

### Dense Brush — Overgrown Ruin

**Living Wall Crawler** — *Pure native fauna.* A vine-creature that scales vertical stone,
ambushing from above — a real, if unsettling, plant-and-animal hybrid biology this brush has
apparently produced more than once.

**Animated Vine** — *Genesis-adjacent.* A writhing plant lash given hostile will, whipping
with a discipline more martial than botanical.

**Ruin Wraith** — *Genesis-adjacent.* A ghost of a fallen settlement, lingering in broken
halls — grief-grey, patient, considerably older than the ruin it haunts.

*Old temple ground. The Bound consider this the most solemn of the four zones; the*

**Mosquito Swarm** — *Pure native fauna.* A cloud of blood-hungry insects, overwhelming

**Mud Lurker** — *Pure native fauna.* A bog predator rising from thick mud to ambush —

### Mirror Pond — Fog Shelf

**Cold Creeper** — *Pure native fauna.* A chill-loving predator of damp caves, patient in

### Spring — Grotto

**Ink Stalker** — *Pure native fauna.* A shadowy hunter moving through dark water,

### Spring — Blue Vent Basin

**Vent Sprite** — *Genesis-adjacent.* A playful but dangerous spirit of cave vents,

**Lion Fish** — *Pure native fauna.* A venomous spined fish, beautiful, slow, and painfully

**Mist Phantom** — *Genesis-adjacent.* A formless spirit of deep mist that drifts through
attacks and confuses foes.

### Water Temple — Flooded Forecourt

**Forecourt Guard** — *Techno-echo.* A disciplined guard of the flooded court, an

**Depth Drainer** — *Pure native fauna.* An abyssal creature that saps life from the deep
— a genuinely effective predator of the dark water, nothing more.

**Tide Sentinel** — *Techno-echo.* An armored watcher of tidal passages, holding ground
against the current with a stillness that hasn't tired once across generations of divers who've

### Water Temple — Aqueduct Nave

**Pressure Monk** — *Pure native fauna.* An ascetic trained in crushing depth,

**Cascade Rider** — *Pure native fauna.* A rider of falling water and rapids, fast, fluid, and
relentless — built by this channel, if not for it.

**Aqueduct Warden** — *Techno-echo.* A guardian of ancient water channels older than
the temple raised around them — armored, immovable, and by every account older still than
either.

### Water Temple — The Drowned Throne

**Still Gazer** — *Genesis-adjacent.* A meditative watcher of unmoving pools, reading foes
and striking with intent that borders on foreknowledge.

**Undertow Shade** — *Genesis-adjacent.* A ghost dragged beneath the current, pulling
and striking from below — grief-grey like every wraith-kin in this world, but colder.

**Throne Revenant** — *Genesis-adjacent.* An undead monarch risen from a sunken
throne, commanding death with royal fury — the only other creature in the whole bestiary to
carry a Warlord's bearing besides the Forest's Vigil Alpha, and the only one anyone ever
thought to give an actual throne.

## TALLY, FOR YOUR OWN SENSE OF THE BALANCE

Roughly a third of the bestiary now reads as plain, warm, native wildlife. A meaningful slice —
concentrated in wolves, hounds, and their kin — carries the feral-Earth-stock thread, which
should feel like discovering a family resemblance the more of them a player encounters
across zones. And the techno-echo tier has shrunk down to where it actually earns attention:
constructs, undead, spirits, mimics, and the handful of Rift-lineage creatures already locked
into the Hollowing document. Nothing in this bestiary announces which bucket it's in. That's
the whole point.
