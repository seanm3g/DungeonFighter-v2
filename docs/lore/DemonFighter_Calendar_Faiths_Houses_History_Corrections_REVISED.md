## 2. Layer 1 — The Ten Faiths of the First Child

Spread across ~6,000 years of in-world history rather than clustered in the post-Antarkeia present. Ground truth (creator-only, never surfaces in-world): demons are humanity's own extracted emotion, launched at the planet as a weapon, that crashed and now walks around wearing the wreckage it landed with.

| Faith | Founded | Status (present, ~YT 275) | Spectrum position |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Long Grief** | ~BYT 5800, Antem-origin | Antem-only, and the Antem are long extinct — survives today only through relics, pictographs, and a garbled echo absorbed into the Ash Vigil (below); no living practitioners at any point close to the present | Close — animist "land remembers its wounds" |
| **The Eye Unclosed** *(renamed from "the coal still watches" concept)* | ~BYT 4200, garbled Precursor-adjacent oral tradition tied to Antem legends of the Black Mun | Extinct by ~BYT 1000; survives only as the idiom **"the Eye doesn't close"** (inescapable consequence) | Moderate-far — demons as judgment of a watching sky-relic |
| **The Twenty-Three Unmakings** *(renamed from "Nine Unmakings" — reducing repeated use of the number nine project-wide)* | ~BYT 2600–2400, doomsday-counting cult | Collapsed under its own ridicule by ~BYT 2100 — the Zoroastrianism-shaped rise-and-fall example | Far — pure numerology, no real theory |
| **The Long Fast tradition** | ~BYT 1400, the desert Prophet | Fragments survive in modern hunting-order language, source unremembered | Middle — demon theory beside the point; practice as personal penance |
| **The unnamed mother church** (pre-schism) | Early in the Age of Man, roughly five thousand years before Year Zero (redated 2026-09-11 — see the Quiet Split entry in Deep History, which describes a schism "within a decade" of this church's founding) | Survived until the Consolidated Faith schism | Far-ish — sincere early divine-testing doctrine |

## 4. Layer 3 — The Four Historical Threads

**Iskrel Sixhold** *(fully replaces the discarded "Kaelen Voss" placeholder — new name, new backstory)*. Scattering Wars era — Antem-internal conflict, deep prehistory (see Deep History's relocated Scattering Wars trio at the end of "The Age of Clans," which explicitly ties Iskrel Sixhold's early tactics to the Skirmish at Yalume Ford, fought between two Antem clans). **Species corrected 2026-09-11: Antem, not Kin** — Kin is cut from current canon entirely, and the Yalume Ford cross-reference confirms Antem regardless. A young Antem commander who conquers six rival holds in under a decade and dies young of an unnamed illness; his territorial empire fractures on his death, but his arbitration norms — **the Unbroken Terms** *(renamed from "the Sixfold Accords" to avoid stacking two number-six references)* — persist only as inherited Faerl legend and idiom, filtered down through millennia of myth-drift the same way the rest of Antem history survives; not as living tradition among any present-day community, Antem or otherwise.

**Ford at Wrenfield — YT 180.** Kester Dray's horse stumbles on buried Antem-era technology at a ford in Wrenfield (Thorne territory) and throws him fatally. This is the sole, unmodified canonical account of his death. **Not connected to the White Wind** (see below — an earlier draft incorrectly conflated the two; they are fully separate events now).

**The White Wind — Landfall Centuries, the Glass Plains** *(date resolved 2026-09-16)*. A wholly separate event and character: **Renna Chases the Mirage**, a Farhand guide leading refugees across the Glass Plains, encounters a sourceless bone-white wind that drains color from everything it touches. Three of her party vanish without trace; she alone emerges strange, fearless in an unsettling way, and never speaks of the loss again. The idiom **"walked through the White Wind"** survives to the present, detached from her name, describing someone changed by loss in an unnatural rather than resilient way.

**The Smoke Year War & the Forgehand Rising** *(renamed from "the Ashfall War" / "the Ashheel Rising")* **— YT 95–103**, post-Antarkeia (corrected from an earlier mis-dated pre-Antarkeia draft; the triggering Colehurst Dominion is explicitly "since Consolidation"). Aldous Colehurst overreaches on conscription and forge-tithes; three vassal houses break, an eight-year war burns Fire-zone territory (genuine ash-fall, hence the old name — now retired for density reasons), and the Dominion collapses when its own capital burns. The internal collapse is led by a low-born forge-hand, now renamed **Garrick Forgehand** *(surname changed from "Ashheel" — the earlier retained Ash- exception is no longer locked)*, whose name the Rising now takes directly.

**Halric Colcross & House Colcross.** The "formerly Ninefinger" origin is retired. Halric is heir of **House Colcross**, a prominent non-paramount house now formally added as a **Thorne vassal** (motto: *"We raise what isn't ours to keep"* / sigil: a single roof over two hearths). His bond with Kester Dray is explained by **the Shared Roof** — a cross-house fostering institution formalized at **the Concord of the Four Hearths (YT 104)**, a postwar summit of all four paramount houses held on Thorne ground in the immediate aftermath of the Smoke Year War, meant to prevent another paramount house unraveling in isolation. Administered mainly by Thorne since. This explains both why Kester Dray was in Wrenfield at his death and why Halric was traveling with him, and gives a concrete reason for the commoners' wrongful blame (neither man was recognized on sight; the court, which knew the fostering history, never doubted Halric).

**Reusable tool unlocked by this thread:** any two houses with an unusually close or trusting cross-territory bond can be explained as having gone through the Shared Roof together, without inventing a bespoke reason each time.
