# DEMON FIGHTER
## Addendum to Species Canon: Ark 2's Scattering

*A breadcrumb, not a rewrite. This does not replace or alter the existing "Faerl — ORIGIN" paragraph in `DemonFighter_Species_Canon_2026-08.docx`. It clarifies a detail that paragraph left implicit.*

---

## What this resolves

The Deep History file's new Faerl origin material (§0, the Ossmoor/Faerling/Long Cold thread) initially read as a direct contradiction of Species Canon's existing line: *"Descendants of Ark 2 — humans who refused emotional extraction and were sent to Faerlan as a consequence or by choice."* Species Canon says Ark 2 was sent to Faerlan. The new material had Ark 2 landing on Ossmoor instead.

## The fix

Ark 2 was a deportation vessel from a faction that didn't care where it landed (established in `DemonFighter_Reference_CONSOLIDATED.md` — formerly `DemonFighter_Reference.docx`: *"Ark 2 carried humans who refused emotional extraction — the most tenacious survivors of labor camp wars"* — no claim of a deliberate destination). On arrival, it scattered: small, disconnected pockets across multiple landmasses, avoiding Faerlan mostly by indifference rather than design, since Faerlan was already the one continent with an established Antem/Vessel civilization worth steering clear of. This also explains why Ark 1 landed there and Ark 2 didn't — Antarkeia was the deliberate, prepared landing; Ark 2 was the leftover nobody bothered aiming.

**Species Canon's "sent to Faerlan" stands, unedited.** It isn't wrong — Faerlan is where the largest surviving branch of Ark 2 eventually converged, generations later, after the Ossmoor pocket's Long Cold migration (~BYT 1000). From the perspective of everyone who's lived there since, "sent to Faerlan" and "eventually converged on Faerlan" read the same. The existing line requires no correction, only this addendum sitting alongside it for anyone who needs the fuller picture.

## What this opens up

Other Ark 2 pockets — on Kessara, the Vantry Isles, or Kaeruvai — are now canon-legal, unconfirmed, unexplored hooks. Nothing is drafted for them. A future session could develop one as a "the Faerl aren't the only Ark 2 descendants" reveal, or leave them permanently unconfirmed as background texture. Either is fine; this addendum only clears the path, it doesn't commit to using it.

## What this does NOT change

- **Antarkeia remains Ark 1's exclusive landing site.** Nothing above implies Ark 2 could have landed there too — the whole point of the fix is that Ark 2 specifically *avoided* the one continent that already had a prepared, established civilization on it. This should stay explicit if the point ever needs restating: Ark 1 landed on Faerlan because it was sent there deliberately; Ark 2 didn't, because it wasn't.
- **The Deep History file's Ossmoor/Faerling/Long Cold material is unchanged**, just reframed as one branch's story rather than the whole of Ark 2's fate. See that document's §0 for the exact wording.
- **No other species entry, timeline, or locked date is affected.** This is scoped narrowly to the Faerl origin paragraph and nothing else in Species Canon.
