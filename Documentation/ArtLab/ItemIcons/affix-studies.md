# Affix visual studies

Concept sheets generated with built-in imagegen using the approved minimal study as a style reference. These are proposals, not registered production layers or native 16×16 atlases. Full prompts are in `affix-studies-prompts.json`.

- `materials-study-01.png`: all 16 MATERIAL entries from PrefixMaterialQuality.json on a shared sword for comparison. This cosmetic comparison intentionally includes materials outside the sword loot ladder. Wood, Leather, and Cloth are additional code fallback materials, not entries in that JSON pool, and are not shown here.
- `quality-study-01.png`: all 11 QUALITY entries, plus neutral baseline.
- `prefixes-study-01.png`: all 23 ADJECTIVE entries from Modifications.json, plus neutral baseline.
- `suffixes-study-01.png`: 13 basic stat suffixes from StatBonuses.json, plus neutral and combined Accuracy/Ferocity examples. Display labels normalize catalog spellings `of ferosity` to Ferocity and `of Rejuvination` to Rejuvenation; game data is unchanged.

Animal and story suffixes are outside this first core-affix visual pass. No game behavior, catalog data, or full-system demo was changed.

Review notes: generated references can drift from intended component ownership. For example Strength appears on the guard rather than the requested grip band. Before production, lock attachment anchors and enforce pixel masks. Some quality distinctions are intentionally subtle and must be reviewed at native size. Affix art is cosmetic and does not imply additional damage types or mechanics.
